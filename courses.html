Autumn schedule

  • 18:00-18:55: Solo skills
  • 19:00-19:55: Improver course (Level 2)
  • 20:00-20:55: Beginner course (Level 1)
  • 21:00-23:00: Social dancing

This semester we've expanded both our Level 1 and Level 2 curricula. Level 1 now runs over a full semester, with different material in the autumn and spring, so we encourage our members to take it again - especially if you've only recently completed it - to build an even stronger foundation for the Level 2 curriculum.

Level 2 has also been refreshed with more advanced patterns and technique, giving experienced dancers even more opportunities to develop their skills. We can't wait to see familiar faces back on the dance floor - and to welcome lots of new ones!
