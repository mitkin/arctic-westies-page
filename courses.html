Our Courses

Weekly West Coast Swing classes in Tromso, from beginner level to improver, plus solo skills and social dancing.

✨ Registration for the autumn semester is open! ✨

Classes start again in one month. Sign up now, and feel free to invite friends, family, colleagues, or anyone else who might enjoy trying West Coast Swing with us.

Autumn schedule

  • 18:00-18:55: Solo skills
  • 19:00-19:55: Improver course (Level 2)
  • 20:00-20:55: Beginner course (Level 1)
  • 21:00-23:00: Social dancing

This semester we expanded both Level 1 and Level 2. Level 1 now runs through the full semester, with different material in autumn and spring. If you recently finished Level 1, we still recommend taking it again to build a stronger base for Level 2.

Level 2 has also been updated with more advanced patterns and technique, giving experienced dancers more to work on this term. We are looking forward to seeing familiar faces again, and meeting new ones.
